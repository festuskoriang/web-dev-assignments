How to Use SpendWise

  1. Enter your monthly budget and select Set Budget.
  2. Fill in the expense name, amount, and category.
  3. Select Add Expense to record your spending.
  4. Review your balance, totals, and category cards.
  5. Use Delete if you need to remove an expense.
